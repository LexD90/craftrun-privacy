CraftRun Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 June 2026

CraftRun is an offline-first app for runners on film and TV sets. It helps you track drink and meal orders for your crew. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app. The short version: almost everything stays on your phone, and the few things that leave are described in full below.

Who is responsible for the data

CraftRun has no accounts and no login. There is no CraftRun server holding your data. The information you enter (crew names, dietary flags, drinks, orders, notes) is yours, and under UK GDPR you are the data controller for it. CraftRun is a tool you use to do your job, like a notebook.

For the small amount of processing CraftRun itself carries out (sending a catering menu to Google when you turn Cloud AI on, and crash reports if they are enabled), the data controller is Olexa Dunko, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. You can get in touch at craftrun.privacy@icloud.com. You remain the data controller for the crew information you enter, as described above.

What stays on your device

By default, everything you enter stays on your phone and is never transmitted anywhere:

  • Crew names (stored as first name plus initial, for example "Mateus C.")
  • Dietary flags, including allergens and Halal or Kosher markers
  • Drink and meal orders, and any free-text notes
  • Templates, preferences, and settings

This data is encrypted at rest on your device. It is readable only inside the app on your phone. In the rare case the app cannot start its encryption, it tells you straight away and stops saving personal data until you restart, so this information is never written to your device unprotected (see Security below).

The only things that leave your device

1. Cloud AI menu parsing (off by default, opt-in)

If you turn on Cloud AI parsing in Settings, the catering menu text or photo you paste is sent to our parsing service (a Cloudflare server, which forwards it to Google Gemini) so it can be read into a structured list. The request carries an anonymous device ID used only to prevent abuse of the free service. No account, no name.

Your crew's names, orders, dietary flags, and notes are never sent during cloud parsing. Only the catering menu itself is sent. If you leave Cloud AI off, nothing is sent and parsing runs entirely on your device.

The menu you send is used only for that one parse. We keep no copy of it: it passes through our server to Google and is dropped straight away. Google does not use it to train their AI models, and holds the request only for a short time to check for misuse, as set out in Google's Gemini API terms.

2. Crash reports (if enabled in the released build)

If the app crashes, a crash report may be sent to our error-reporting provider (Sentry) so we can fix the problem. Personal data is stripped from these reports before they are sent. We do not use them to track you, and they are not linked to your identity.

We do not use advertising, we do not sell data, and we do not run usage analytics that profile you.

Special-category data (religion and health)

Halal and Kosher flags can reveal a person's religion, and allergen flags relate to health. Under UK GDPR these are special-category data. Because this data stays on your device and you collect it directly from the people it concerns, you should make sure each person is happy for you to record it. If you ever share a roster with another runner, ask each person's permission first, and use the per-person hide-from-share option for anyone who does not want their details shared.

Sharing a roster with another runner

If you share a roster device-to-device, you are sending crew names and dietary flags to that runner. They then become responsible for that data on their own device. Only share with people who need it, and only with the crew's permission.

Children's data

If your roster includes anyone under 18, extra care applies under UK GDPR. The data still stays on your device, and you remain responsible for it.

Keeping data, and deleting it

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete individual people, clear your roster, or delete the app, which removes all of it. There is no copy held anywhere else for us to delete.

Security

Data is encrypted at rest on your device. In the rare event the app cannot start that encryption, it warns you immediately and will not save personal data (such as names, notes, or dietary flags) until you restart, so this information is never written to your device unencrypted. We also rely on your phone's own protection (your passcode and device encryption), so please keep a passcode set. If your phone is lost or stolen, use your phone's Find My or remote-wipe feature.

Third parties

We use these services, and only for the purposes above:

  • Cloudflare and Google Gemini: only when you turn on Cloud AI parsing, and only for the menu text or photo you paste.
  • Sentry: only for crash reports, with personal data removed.

Where your data goes

When Cloud AI parsing is on, the menu text or photo is processed by Google on its own infrastructure, which may be outside the UK. Google's terms allow this data to be handled in any country where it keeps facilities. The transfer happens only for that one menu, only while you have the feature switched on, and relies on Google's own safeguards for moving data between countries. If you keep Cloud AI off, nothing is sent and no transfer takes place.

The same applies to crash reports, if they are enabled: they go to Sentry, whose servers may also be outside the UK, with the same reliance on the provider's safeguards. Personal data is removed before a crash report is sent.

Your rights

Because the data lives on your device and we hold no copy, you are in direct control of it. You can view, edit, or delete any of it inside the app at any time.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
